Finflow is an ultra-private, offline-first personal finance manager designed to give you complete control and deep visibility over your money without ever compromising your data.

🔒 100% PRIVATE & OFFLINE-FIRST
- Zero cloud tracking, zero accounts required, zero third-party telemetry.
- All your financial data is stored locally on your device with military-grade AES-256 SQLCipher database encryption.
- Built-in biometric lock (Fingerprint / Face Unlock).

📊 POWERFUL 4-DIMENSIONAL TRACKING
Every movement is categorized along 4 clear dimensions:
1. Category & Subcategory: Where did the money go?
2. Entity / Context: Work, Family, Personal, Vacation, etc.
3. Method: Cash, Debit, Credit Card, Bank Transfer, MB WAY.
4. Classification: Fixed vs. Variable expenses.

⚡ SMART PREFILL & RECURRING ENGINE

- Wealth & investment portfolio tracking: PPRs, ETFs, stocks, crypto, and emergency funds.
- Tax optimization insights (e.g. Portuguese PPR Art. 21 EBF benefits).

💼 COMPLETE DATA SOVEREIGNTY
- Instant JSON & CSV export/import for encrypted local backups.
- No ads, no paywalls, no tracking.
